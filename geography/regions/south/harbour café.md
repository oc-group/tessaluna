# The Harbour Café

A small café at the root of the pier in [[South Harbour]]. Passengers wait here for the ferry,
and fishermen from the North come down on market days.

## Menu

| Item | Price (shells) |
|---|---:|
| Coffee | 1 |
| Lantern-grass tea | 1 |
| Lighthouse bread, slice | 2 |
| Spinner soup, bowl | 4 |

The soup and the bread follow the island [[recipes]].

Opening hours follow the ferry timetable: first departure minus one hour, last arrival plus
thirty minutes.
