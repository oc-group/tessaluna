# Old Town

Old Town is the largest village on the island. It grew up around a covered market, the
**Salt Loggia**, where salt, wool and fish are still traded today.

## Quarters

### Net Quarter

Narrow lanes where fishing nets are mended. The houses have green doors.

### Loom Quarter

Wool from [[highfold|Highfold]] comes here to be woven.

### Loggia Quarter

The main square, where [[lantern night|Lantern Night]] begins.

## Why "old"?

Because it is older than South Harbour, although younger than Highfold. Islanders say it is
*old in name, young at heart*.
