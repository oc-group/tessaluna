# North Harbour

North Harbour belongs to the fishermen. Boats leave at dawn and are back before the Spinner
picks up.

## The catch

| Fish | Season | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Moon mackerel | summer | caught at night |
| Green rock mullet | all year | |
| Striped octopus | winter | protected in spring |

Whatever is not sold at the market ends up in the [[recipes]] of the [[food/README|island kitchen]].

Ferries do not call here: they use [[South Harbour]]
([[geography/regions/south/harbour|on the other side of the island]]).
