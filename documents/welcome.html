Welcome to Tessaluna

An island in six slides

Greet the group and point out the map on the wall.

Where it is

In the middle of the Vessaro Sea, an hour and a half by ferry from Vessara Coast.

Show the crescent shape: Long Point to the north, Short Point to the south.

Two regions

North

  • Mount Lantern, 712 m
  • Highfold
  • North Harbour

South

  • Old Town
  • South Harbour
  • Eastern Salt Pans

The lighthouse

Lit in 212 after the Landing: two flashes every ten seconds.

Lantern Night

On the longest night of the year every door has a lantern. At midnight the lighthouse goes dark for one minute.

Mention that visitors are welcome in the procession, and that the soup is free.

Getting here

The Vessaro Star and the Little Gull sail to South Harbour.
