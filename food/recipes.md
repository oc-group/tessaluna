# Recipes

## Spinner soup

Served on [[lantern night|Lantern Night]] and at [[harbour café|the Harbour Café]].

**Serves 4**

- 600 g mixed fish from [North Harbour](../geography/regions/north/villages/harbour.md)
- 2 potatoes
- 1 onion
- a pinch of salt from the pans
- wild herbs

1. Soften the onion.
2. Add the diced potatoes and cover with water.
3. After 20 minutes, add the fish.
4. Cook 10 more minutes and serve with toasted bread.
