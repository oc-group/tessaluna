# Silver snail

A small shell-less snail, three centimetres long, that gives off a faint silver glow at night.
On damp nights the meadows of the South look as if they were scattered with stars.

It feeds on [[flora#lantern grass|lantern grass]] and is the favourite prey of the
[[spinner falcon]].

According to [[lantern night|a legend]], the island's first lanterns were jars full of silver
snails.
