# Spinner falcon

A small grey bird of prey that nests on the western cliffs. It uses the Spinner to hang
motionless in the air for minutes on end, then drops onto its prey.

Islanders take it as a sign of fair weather: *still falcon, still sea*.

Prey: [[silver snail]], [[copper crab]].
