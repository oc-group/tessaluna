# Overview

Tessaluna is about eighteen kilometres long and, at its widest, barely five across. From above
it looks like a crescent moon: the northern tip is called **Long Point** and carries
[[lighthouse|the lighthouse]], the southern tip is **Short Point**.

## The lay of the land

The island has two regions: the windswept, pine-covered [[geography/regions/north/README|North]]
and the milder [[geography/regions/south/README|South]], home to the [[old town]]. Between them
lies **Mirror Lake**, fed by the springs of Mount Lantern.

Along the eastern shore stretch the **Eastern Salt Pans**, home of the [[copper crab]] and a
favourite hunting ground of the [[spinner falcon]].
