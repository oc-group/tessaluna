# The stone whale

The elders say that Tessaluna is not an island at all but a sleeping whale, and that Mount
Lantern is its back. The whale will only wake when the last lantern on the island goes out,
which, they say, is why [[lantern night|Lantern Night]] is never skipped.

The sea stacks called **the Three Sisters** ([[coasts and rocks]]) are said to be its fins.
