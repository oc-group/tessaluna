# The keeper's cottage

The cottage at the foot of [[lighthouse|the lighthouse]] has two rooms and a lean-to for firewood.
Since the light became automatic it has been a museum of the sea.

## In the museum

- The old lamp, with its brass reflector
- The keeper's [[lighthouse-log|log books]]
- A model of the *Vessaro Star*, the first ferry
- A jar that, according to the guides, once held silver snails

Open on weekends from spring to autumn. Entry is one shell.
